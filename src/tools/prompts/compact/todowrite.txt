Replaces the session task list. Use it to plan work with 3 or more steps
and to record progress: keep exactly one item in_progress at a time, and
mark items completed immediately when done. Skip it for trivial one-step
tasks.

Each item has content (short task description), status (pending |
in_progress | completed), and an optional id. Send the WHOLE list every
call — it replaces the previous list.

Example:
{"todos": [
  {"id": "1", "content": "Add the parser", "status": "completed"},
  {"id": "2", "content": "Wire it into main", "status": "in_progress"}
]}
